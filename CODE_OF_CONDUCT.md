# Code of Conduct

Kintio welcomes participants with different levels of experience,
backgrounds, and viewpoints. Discussion may be direct and disagreement is
allowed, but it must remain focused on the problem and evidence rather than the
person.

## Encouraged behavior

- Describe problems clearly, and give others room to add information or correct
  a conclusion.
- Give newcomers the context they need without requiring them to understand the
  entire internal architecture first.
- Accept evidence-based technical feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and correct
  them promptly.
- Protect the privacy of channel users, operators, and other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language, or sustained
  personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's identity, contact details, conversations, media,
  or other private information without permission.
- Deliberately publishing credentials, databases, unredacted logs, or security
  vulnerability details.
- Spam, impersonation, phishing, malicious links or attachments, repeated
  substantially identical content, reopening settled topics, or evading an
  interaction limit or block.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or retaliating against someone who reports
  a problem.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may minimize or remove unsafe content, close or lock discussions,
restrict or block accounts, and report abuse to GitHub. A low-impact first
incident normally receives one clear boundary when practical. Threats, doxxing,
credential disclosure, phishing, and malicious software may be removed and
blocked immediately without a prior warning. Enforcement considers impact,
repetition, attempts to evade controls, and corrective action.

Do not report a conduct incident together with another person's private
information in a public issue. Email
[gkxie@qq.com](mailto:gkxie@qq.com). If email is unavailable, open only a
request for a private contact channel and omit the person's identity and the
incident details.

This policy applies in Kintio-managed spaces and to off-project conduct that
directly targets a participant because of their Kintio participation or
directly threatens the project's security.
